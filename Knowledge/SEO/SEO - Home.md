## Cross-Cutting Principles

Patterns that recur across all four pages:

| Principle | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Originality is the price of entry.** Scaled or aggregated content without something new is spam risk and does not get quoted. Each page needs one thing only you have: a study, your own number, a quiz or calculator. | Info gain study (step 5-6), one original stat per statistics page, one interactive element per SaaS page |
| **But coverage comes first.** Complete coverage of what the SERP already answers is the floor; uniqueness without it performed worst. | AI Search Visibility (Borja data) |
| **Write standalone, quotable units.** One stat per line with its source; paragraphs and table rows that answer a question alone; specific numbers over adjectives. | AI Search Visibility, Statistics Pages |
| **Go where incumbents won't or can't.** Alternatives and vs pages, integration pages, statistics/trends SERPs, GBP features competitors ignore. | SaaS Page System, Statistics Pages, Local Playbook |
| **Consistency across the web beats cleverness on one page.** Same claim in every bio, directory and review site; same NAP and categories in GBP. | AI Search Visibility, Local Playbook |
| **Freshness is a maintained asset.** Quarterly refreshes, yearly statistics updates with the year in title not URL, weekly GBP posts and photos. | All pages |
| **Claude gathers, humans judge.** Agents collect SERPs, competitor data and drafts; humans own brand arguments, study design, and pre-publish checks for what automation silently dropped. | SaaS Page System, Local Playbook, info gain playbook |
| **Backlinks matter less than assumed for the entry point.** AI Overview citation barely tracked links; small sites hold ~24% of statistics SERPs. Authority still gates hard head terms. | AI Search Visibility, Statistics Pages, SaaS Page System |

> [!question]
> Open conflict: whether schema markup and content "chunking" help AI citation. One source recommends Organization/FAQ schema; another cites Google's AI optimization guide saying they are not what generative search runs on. Current stance: cheap hygiene, not a lever.
