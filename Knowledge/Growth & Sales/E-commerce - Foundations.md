### Starter tool stack

| Tool | Role in the stack |
|---|---|
| **Shopify** | Store builder / storefront. The course points to a trial offer (3 free days, then a discounted first month) |
| **Minea** | Ad-spy and product-research tool (browsing winning ads, e.g. Facebook ads). Starter and Premium tiers |
