## SEO & AI search

**State:** Active. Four playbooks ingested from X threads and a newsletter; no hands-on results recorded yet.

**What is known:**
- AI answer engines cite brands described consistently across many trusted places; a single page matters less than corroboration.
- Cover everything the top results cover, *then* add one original element (study, proprietary number, tool). Originality without coverage performed worst.
- Low-authority sites win where incumbents don't compete: alternatives / "X vs Y", integrations, `[niche] statistics [year]` (small sites hold ~24% of page one), and neglected Google Business Profile features.
- Claude scales production but silently drops unique elements; Google's scaled content abuse policy targets unoriginal volume. Human review per page.

**Open questions:**
- Does schema / chunking help generative search? Ramonov says yes; Borja cites Google saying no.

**Key sources:** [[SEO - Home]] | [[SEO - AI Search Visibility]] | [[SEO - Statistics Pages Without Backlinks]]

## Growth & Sales

**State:** Active. Outreach, positioning, GTM and early-distribution playbooks ingested; e-commerce is a stub.

**What is known:**
- Start from one specific buyer and their exact words. Research is the work; no specific hook, no send.
- Give value before pitching, where buyers already gather. Capped, low volume; add one channel at a time. Launch spikes are not strategy.
- Agents research and draft; a human approves anything that sends, spends or prices. Retention and win-back before acquisition.

**Open questions:**
- Blank vs personalised LinkedIn connection requests, and links in posts: sources disagree (reconciled on [[Sales - Outbound Outreach]], unverified).
- Automated sending vs manual DMs given platform ban risk.

**Key sources:** [[Growth & Sales - Home]] | [[Sales - Outbound Outreach]] | [[Growth - First Users Without Ad Spend]]

## AI & Agents

**State:** Active. Claude Code workflow, orchestration, MVP rules, AI video and MCP reference ingested.

**What is known:**
- Context is the scarce resource: externalise it into files (`CLAUDE.md`, roadmaps, review standards, vaults) and partition it across sub-agents and worktrees.
- Delegate small tasks with an explicit definition of done; agents verify themselves (run the app, CI, AI review) before human judgement. The human shifts from writer to reviewer.
- Package repeated prompts as skills, connect tools via MCP, and add guardrails (spend approval, cost caps, permission tiers) that loosen with trust.

**Open questions:**
- Skip-permissions for worker agents: [[AI Agents - Multi-Agent Orchestration]] uses it, [[Claude Code - Working System]] warns against it.
- Is Claude a useful code reviewer, or is Codex better? Sources disagree.

**Key sources:** [[AI & Agents - Home]] | [[Claude Code - Working System]] | [[AI Agents - Multi-Agent Orchestration]]
