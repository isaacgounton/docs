2.5 years of app scaling, distribution, and marketing sauce in 2 mins (Part 1):

(BOOKMARK THIS)

1\. When you evaluate a niche to build an app in, look for these 4 factors to predict your upside:

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HOpA5EXWsAAtCvA?format=jpg&name=large) ![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HOpA6zQW4AAPYNf?format=jpg&name=large)

---

1\. An expanding market

2\. Cult-like audiences

3\. Cheap creators

4\. Existing demand

---

2\. If you’re running influencer marketing campaigns, skip household names and nano-creators.

Both are overpriced, for different reasons.

Sign mid-tier creators ONLY to start (10-100k followers with good engagement)

---

3\. Identify the highest leverage tasks that actually generate revenue

and just do as much of those as humanly possible.

---

4\. Your messaging in ads, UGC, and influencer campaigns should be outcome-focused, not feature focused.

Cal AI sold the body of your dreams, NOT calorie tracking.

---

5\. You're wasting your time if you're trying to copy EXACTLY what we did to scale Cal AI.

Take the frameworks, use Cal AI as a case study, and adapt it to YOUR app.

---

6\. Every app that blew up in the last two years had a magic moment in its marketing.

If you can't describe your magic moment in one sentence, your marketing problem isn't a budget problem.

---

7\. NEVER run a free trial until you’ve proven your app is something people are actually willing to pay for out the gate.

---

8\. Almost every successful app is an ENTERTAINMENT tool disguised as a productivity tool, educational tool, etc.

Gamify as much of your app as possible and make it something your customers want to keep coming back to because it’s fun.

---

9\. Never underestimate the conversion power of cult-like audiences.

Arguably the most important factor when picking a niche to build an app in the B2C space.

---

10\. I personally would avoid lifetime app plans at all costs for a few reasons:

\- there can be legal headaches to deal with

\- can complicate things when it comes to acquisition

\- generally doesn’t result in that much more revenue than annual plans

---

11\. If you really want to win in the app space, price your product for mindshare first, and margin second.

This helps you become the default option when people think of the niche your product lives in.

---

12\. It takes more skill to run UGC and slideshows profitably than influencers and paid ads.

---

13\. You’ll never regret hiring a cracked designer, it’s such an underrated asset for any team.

AI continues to get better with design and creative taste, but don’t sleep on having a Canva killer.
